About

Hey, I'm Vishrut, a Computer and Communication Engineering student at MIT Manipal (class of 2027).

I work on the low-level side of graphics and systems: path tracing (BDPT, photon mapping, ReSTIR), PBR shading and GPU optimisation in C++ and CUDA, plus a capability-based RISC-V microkernel in Rust. Most of my free time goes into open source: the RPCS3 and PCSX2 emulators, the PrismLauncher Minecraft launcher, and lately Blender itself.

I'm also a 3D artist. As Blender Head at VARISE I lead the 3D and VR pipeline; our team placed Top 8 of ~2,000 at the XR Creator Hackathon. The renders below cover simulations, motion graphics, environments and VR sets.

This summer I interned at Engineers India Limited, building a RAG assistant for their employee dashboard, and my team finished in the top 5% of 10,000+ teams in the Amazon ML Challenge 2026.
